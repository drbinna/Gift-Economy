The Gift Economy
A bidding game where winning arms the player next to you.
Shuffling the prize deck

Everyone holds cards numbered 1 to 7. Nine prizes come out one at a time.

  1. 1
    Bid. A prize flips over. Everyone secretly plays one card face down, then all reveal at once.
  2. 2
    Cancel. Any number played by more than one person is crossed out. The highest number still standing takes the prize.
  3. 3
    Give. The card you played is not discarded. It goes to the player on your left and joins their hand.

So winning costs you your best card and hands it straight to a rival. And playing a 1 loses the round on purpose, but clogs their hand with junk.

Set up the table

Name everyone playing and leave the rest as bots. One device gets passed around, so each person only ever sees their own hand.

Round log

    Runs whole games between bots and reports what the rules actually produced. Change a number, run it again, and see which way the game moves.

    Bots bid roughly in proportion to how good the prize is against the prizes still unseen, with three temperaments mixed in: one bids high, one shades down a step to duck ties, one plays it straight. They are not great players. They are consistent enough to tell you whether a rule change moved the game.
